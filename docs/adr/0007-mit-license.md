# 0007: MIT license

Status: accepted

## Decision

The project is released under the MIT license.

## Why

It is the lowest-friction choice for adoption. Commercial services such as hosting remain possible on top of an MIT codebase, and relicensing later is hard once others have contributed.

## Considered

Apache-2.0 (adds a patent grant) and AGPL-3.0 (blocks closed hosted forks, but deters some contributors and users).
